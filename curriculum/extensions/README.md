# Optional extensions

Choose extensions by customer need, prerequisites, licensing and hardware access. Their original content and incomplete/validation states are preserved. They are not additional required stages of the core learning path.

- [Bare metal](bare-metal/README.md): Source labs remain under development; their existing sandbox/BMC access requirements apply.
- [Virtualization](virtualization/README.md): The existing eight-GPU passthrough lab retains its specialized hardware prerequisites; additional original labs remain unwritten.
- [Advanced Kubernetes](advanced-kubernetes/README.md): Hosted control planes, networking and DRA retain their individual prerequisites and mixed historical validation states.
- [Licensed orchestration](licensed-orchestration/README.md): Run:AI requires the documented licensed control plane and configured environment; it is an alternative to the open KAI path.
- [ML platforms](ml-platforms/README.md): Jupyter, vector databases, Kubeflow and MLflow keep their complete labs and storage/operator requirements.
- [Compliance](compliance/README.md): The FIPS exercise retains its image/crypto gates; a stock-host smoke result is not compliance certification.
- [Advanced inference](advanced-inference/README.md): TensorRT-LLM keeps its separate driver/container/GPU compatibility path.
- [HPC](hpc/README.md): Slurm keeps its multi-node, host support and RWX storage prerequisites.
